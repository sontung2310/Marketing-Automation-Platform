Architecture / C4 / PoC

Marketing Automation Platform

Two-agent architecture: the Strategy Agent decides what the business should do; the Execution Agent turns explicit requests into marketing deliverables and future actions. Background data pipelines populate an Intelligence DB, while agents retrieve information on demand through an Intelligence MCP/API.

Architecture Rules

The core boundaries to keep stable while the PoC grows.

01
Two agentsStrategy answers “what should we do?” Execution answers “do this task for me.”
02
Pipelines are infrastructureCompetitor monitoring, trend collection and influencer discovery populate the Intelligence DB. They are not agents.
03
On-demand intelligenceThe agents decide when they need data and query semantic tools through the Intelligence MCP/API.
04
Skills teach the workMarketing Skills provide reusable methods such as product marketing, content strategy, copywriting and SEO.
05
Mock firstFor the PoC, the Intelligence DB can contain realistic mock competitor, trend and influencer records.
06
Execution can expand laterEmail, CRM, CMS and social integrations can be added behind MCP/API without changing the two-agent model.
